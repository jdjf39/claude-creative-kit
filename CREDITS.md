# Credits

The skills in `skills/` were written by the owner of this kit, from real projects and real feedback.

`install.sh` also fetches these skills by other people, straight from their own repositories, so you always
get their latest version under their own licence. Nothing of theirs is copied into this repository.

| skill | what it is for | author / source | licence |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| hyperframes (+ media-use and friends) | turns HTML into rendered video; the render engine | [heygen-com/hyperframes](https://github.com/heygen-com/hyperframes) | Apache-2.0 |
| gsap-* | the GSAP animation library, used in videos and websites | [greensock/gsap-skills](https://github.com/greensock/gsap-skills) | MIT |
| impeccable | design critique and polish for any interface | [pbakaus/impeccable](https://github.com/pbakaus/impeccable) | Apache-2.0 |
| ui-ux-pro-max | searchable library of styles, palettes, font pairings | [nextlevelbuilder/ui-ux-pro-max-skill](https://github.com/nextlevelbuilder/ui-ux-pro-max-skill) | MIT |
| taste-skill | stops websites looking templated | [Leonxlnx/taste-skill](https://github.com/Leonxlnx/taste-skill) | MIT |
| cinetic | cinematic motion design from code | [Leonxlnx/cinetic](https://github.com/Leonxlnx/cinetic) | MIT |
| motion-director | plans, storyboards and scores motion videos | [systembuiltbyaj/motion-director](https://github.com/systembuiltbyaj/motion-director) | MIT |
| motionmaxxing | directs motion-graphics films that do not look AI made | [Tejashmakwana/motionmaxxing](https://github.com/Tejashmakwana/motionmaxxing) | Apache-2.0 |
| playwright-cli | lets Claude drive a browser to test pages | [microsoft/playwright-cli](https://github.com/microsoft/playwright-cli) | Apache-2.0 |

Bundled with `reel-editor`: the fonts Montserrat, Anton, Inter and JetBrains Mono (SIL Open Font License) and
the OpenCV Zoo face models YuNet (MIT) and SFace (Apache-2.0).

Tools the kit installs: ffmpeg, yt-dlp, whisper.cpp, uv, Node.js.
